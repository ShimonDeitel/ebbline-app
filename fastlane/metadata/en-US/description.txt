Freelance income does not arrive on a schedule, but budgeting apps pretend it does. They build a tidy monthly plan around a paycheck you do not have, so the number they show is either too cautious or quietly wrong. Ebbline shows one number you can trust: what is actually safe to spend today, counting only money that has really arrived.

Log income when a client actually pays you, and add bills as they come in. The Flow screen shows your safe-to-spend figure on an animated tide gauge, a liquid meter that visibly rises when money lands and recedes when bills come due. Drag the float line, a marker on the timeline for when your next payment is expected, to simulate a late payment. With Pro, the safe-to-spend number recalculates live while your finger is still moving. The Ledger keeps every income event and bill in one place, and the Forecast screen answers the question you are already asking in your head: am I still okay if that invoice is late?

The free tier includes one income stream, unlimited bills, and the core safe-to-spend number: income actually received minus bills already due, never aspirational. Ebbline Pro is an auto-renewing monthly subscription at $4.99 per month. It unlocks unlimited income streams, bill-aware recalculation that reserves for bills due before your next expected payment, and the AI shortfall forecaster, which reads your recent income and upcoming bills and warns you in plain English about the exact date and amount of any projected shortfall.
