Ebbline Privacy Policy

Freelance Cash Flow

Effective July 17, 2026

The short version

Ebbline has no accounts, no bank connection, no ads, no trackers, and no analytics SDKs. We collect as little as technically possible.

What stays on your device

Every income entry, bill, and expected payment you log is stored only in this app's local storage on your device. We cannot see it, there is no server-side account, and deleting the app deletes it. Ebbline never connects to your bank — every figure comes from what you typed in.

What leaves your device

If you use the Pro AI shortfall forecaster, the dollar amounts and dates of your recent income and upcoming bills are sent over an encrypted connection to our AI processing service so it can write your forecast sentence. We do not send your name, email, bank details, or any account identifier — Ebbline has none to send. That request is processed to generate your answer and is not stored by us afterward.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple provides us anonymous receipts to unlock Pro features.

Children

Ebbline is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
